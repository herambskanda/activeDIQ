# <Reset your password>

You can reset your work password yourself, any time, without calling {{it_team}}.

## What you need

- To have set up self-service first ([Set up self-service](set-up-self-service.md)).
- Your phone, if you chose a phone-based method.

## Steps

1. Go to {{portal_url}}, or select **Reset password** on your computer's sign-in screen.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Confirm it is you with one of the methods you set up.
4. Enter a new password. The page shows the rules your password must meet.
5. Select **Reset password**.

You can now sign in with your new password. On a laptop away from the office, connect to
{{vpn_name}} first if your organisation uses one.

## If it does not work

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "We could not confirm it is you." | Try another method, or contact {{it_team}}. |
| Your account is locked. | Wait <n> minutes or contact {{it_team}}. |
